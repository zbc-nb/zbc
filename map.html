About this data: everything about your usage is real, tracked by PromptPrint in this browser. Grid carbon intensity is approximate (Ember / Our World in Data, 2023). In reality the data center that answers a prompt may be in another country, so the "your country" figure is an estimate.

Comparing with other people's usage needs an opt-in sharing server, which isn't built yet (see the README). Until then, only your own data is shown.
