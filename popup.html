    How is this calculated?

    Each prompt's electricity = the AI's energy per typical prompt × how long its reply was (longer replies use more). CO₂ = electricity × how much CO₂ your country's power grid emits per kWh.

    Only Google (Gemini) and OpenAI (ChatGPT) publish per-prompt energy figures. Every other AI uses our estimate of 0.3 Wh per prompt, marked "estimate". All numbers are approximate: the true value is likely between half and double.
