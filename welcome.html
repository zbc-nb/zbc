Choose what PromptPrint may track.

PromptPrint estimates the electricity, carbon and cooling water behind your AI chats. It needs your permission to read each AI website. Nothing is tracked until you allow it.

What PromptPrint reads, and what it keeps

  • Reads: the messages on the AI sites you allow, while you use them in Chrome.
  • Keeps: which AI, the date, the task category (like Math or Coding) and message lengths.
  • Never keeps: your chat text. It's sorted into a category on your computer, then thrown away.
  • Never sends: anything. All data stays in this browser.

Your AI platforms

Tick the ones you use. Chrome will ask you to confirm.

Then

  1. Pin PromptPrint: click the puzzle piece next to the address bar, then the pin next to PromptPrint.
  2. Chat as usual. Open the PromptPrint icon to see your footprint.
  3. Want older chats too? Use Import past chat history in the popup.
  4. Change your mind anytime under Your connected platforms in the popup, or in Chrome's extension settings.
