Choose what Evergreen may track.

Evergreen estimates the electricity, carbon and cooling water behind your AI chats. It needs your permission to read each AI website. Nothing is tracked until you allow it.

What Evergreen reads, and what it keeps

  • Reads: the messages on the AI sites you allow, while you use them in Chrome.
  • Keeps: which AI, the date, the task category (like Math or Coding) and message lengths.
  • Never keeps: your chat text. It's sorted into a category on your computer, then thrown away.
  • Sends: nothing, unless you turn on map sharing below. Then only anonymous daily totals leave this browser.

Your AI platforms

Tick the ones you use. Chrome will ask you to confirm.

Then

  1. Pin Evergreen: click the puzzle piece next to the address bar, then the pin next to Evergreen.
  2. Chat as usual. Open the Evergreen icon to see your footprint.
  3. Want older chats too? Use Import past chat history in the popup.
  4. Change your mind anytime under Your connected platforms in the popup, or in Chrome's extension settings.
